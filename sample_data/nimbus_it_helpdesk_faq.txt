NIMBUS RETAIL PVT. LTD. - IT HELPDESK FAQ (Version 3.2, updated August 2026)

Q: How do I contact the IT Helpdesk?
A: Call extension 4357 (H-E-L-P) from any office phone, email helpdesk@nimbusretail.example, or raise a
ticket in the ServiceDesk portal. The Helpdesk is staffed from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST, Monday to Saturday.
Priority 1 incidents (store billing systems down) are handled 24x7 by the on-call engineer.

Q: How do I connect to the VPN?
A: Nimbus uses GlobalProtect VPN. Open the GlobalProtect app and enter the portal address
vpn.nimbusretail.example. Sign in with your Google Workspace account and approve the MFA prompt.
VPN is required to reach the finance ERP and the store inventory dashboards from outside the office.

Q: What is the office Wi-Fi network?
A: Employees connect to the "Nimbus-Corp" network using certificate-based authentication (EAP-TLS);
the certificate is installed automatically on managed laptops. Visitors use "Nimbus-Guest", which needs
a daily access code from the reception desk and is isolated from internal systems.

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: Use the self-service portal at password.nimbusretail.example. You will need your registered mobile number
for the one-time code. If you are locked out after 5 failed attempts, wait 15 minutes or call the Helpdesk.

Q: How often are laptops replaced?
A: Laptops are refreshed on a 4-year cycle. Engineering and Design roles may request an early refresh after
3 years with manager approval. Old devices are wiped with a certified erase tool before they are recycled.

Q: Can I install my own software?
A: Only software from the Self Service catalogue may be installed. Anything else needs an exception request
reviewed by IT Security, which normally takes 3 business days.

Q: What should I do if I lose my laptop or phone?
A: Report it to the Helpdesk and to security@nimbusretail.example within 1 hour. IT will lock and remotely
wipe the device. Laptops are encrypted (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows), so data stays protected.

Q: How do I get access to a shared drive or application?
A: Raise an Access Request in the ServiceDesk portal. Your manager approves it, then the application owner.
Standard access requests are completed within 2 business days.
